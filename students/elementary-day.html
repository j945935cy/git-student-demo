小學生的一天

A Day in an Elementary Student's Life

06:30

起床、刷牙、洗臉，準備上學。

Wake up, brush teeth, wash face, and get ready for school.

07:20

吃早餐，和家人說早安。

Eat breakfast and say good morning to family.

08:00

到學校，和同學一起早自習。

Arrive at school and join morning study with classmates.

10:00

上國語、數學和自然課，認真聽老師講課。

Take Chinese, math, and science classes and listen carefully to the teacher.

12:00

午餐時間，吃營養午餐後休息一下。

Lunch time—eat a healthy meal and take a short rest.

14:00

體育課和社團活動，運動讓身體更健康。

P.E. class and club activities—exercise keeps the body healthy.

16:00

放學回家，先完成作業。

Go home after school and finish homework first.

19:00

吃晚餐、閱讀故事書，和家人聊天。

Have dinner, read storybooks, and chat with family.

21:00

整理書包，準備明天，早睡早起身體好。

Pack the school bag for tomorrow and go to bed early for good health.

快樂學習，健康成長！ | Learn happily, grow healthily!
